# Studio Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

## The short version

- Your workspace (brands, footage, edits, library) **stays on your Mac**. We don't upload it to our servers.
- Our servers hold only what we need to run your account: your email and name, subscription and licence status, waitlist and referral details, and minimal usage and diagnostic data.
- Payments go through Stripe. **We never see or store your full card number.**
- When you use a connected provider (Claude, ElevenLabs, Meta and others), you send your content to that provider under your own account and its own privacy policy.
- **We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.**
- You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time: hello@askstudio.ai.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nicolini Productions LLC, a California company doing business as Studio ("**Studio**", "**we**", "**us**"), collects, uses and shares personal information when you use the Studio Mac app, the website at askstudio.ai, our waitlist, and related services (the "**Service**"). We are the "controller" (or "business") for the information described here. Our [Terms of Service](terms.md) also apply.

## 3. What stays on your Mac

Studio is built to keep your work local. **The following stays on your Mac, and we don't collect it on our servers**:

- your workspace: brands, footage, images, audio, edits, projects, scripts, prompts and library;
- content you generate with your connected providers;
- credentials and tokens for your connected third-party accounts.

When you use a feature that sends content to a provider you've connected (for example, a prompt to Claude, a script to ElevenLabs, or a video to Instagram), the App sends it **directly to that provider under your account**. That provider's terms and privacy policy govern it, not this policy. If you send us files or screenshots for support, we use them only to help you, and delete them when we're done.

## 4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

1. **Provide the Service**: create and secure your account, sign you in, verify licences and seats, enforce device limits, and deliver updates.
2. **Process payments**: run trials and subscriptions, send receipts and renewal reminders, handle failed payments, and prevent fraud.
3. **Run the waitlist and referrals**: hold your place, calculate your position and referral rank, give out invites and rewards, and detect fake sign-ups.
4. **Communicate with you**: send sign-in codes, account and billing notices, and support replies; and, with your permission or where the law allows, product news and marketing.
5. **Keep things safe and working**: diagnose crashes, fix bugs, monitor performance, and detect abuse, security incidents and violations of our Terms.
6. **Improve Studio**: understand, in aggregate, which features are used, so we know what to build. **We don't use your workspace content, and we don't use your personal information to train AI models.**
7. **Meet legal obligations**: tax, accounting, responding to lawful requests, and enforcing our rights.

## 5. Emails

- **Transactional emails** (sign-in codes, receipts, trial and renewal reminders, cancellation confirmations, security and legal notices) are part of the Service. You can't opt out of them while you have an account.
- **Marketing emails** (launch news, tips, offers) are optional. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email hello@askstudio.ai. We act on opt-outs within 10 business days.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

askstudio.ai uses only what it needs to work: browser local storage and essential cookies that remember your waitlist session, referral code and sign-in state, and that prevent abuse. **We don't currently use third-party analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels on the Site.**

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future (for example, a Meta Pixel or a Google tag), we will update this policy first, ask for consent where the law requires it, and give you a way to opt out, including a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link and support for Global Privacy Control (see Section 9).

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. Some Site features, such as waitlist status, may then stop working.

**9.2 No sale or sharing.** **We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising,** as those terms are defined in California law. If this changes (for example, if we add advertising pixels to the Site), we will update this policy beforehand, add a **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link, and honour opt-outs, including browser **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals.

**9.3 Your rights.** Depending on your state, you may have the right to: know and access the personal information we hold about you; get a portable copy of it; correct it; delete it; opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling; and limit the use of sensitive personal information. **We give these rights to all users, wherever they live.**

**9.4 How to exercise them.** Email hello@askstudio.ai from your account email. We'll verify your identity (usually by confirming that email address) and respond within **45 days**, extendable once by 45 days if we tell you. **9.5 No discrimination.** We won't deny you service, charge you a different price, or give you a lower quality of service because you exercised these rights.

**9.6 Shine the Light.** California residents may ask once a year whether we've disclosed personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing. We haven't.

## 11. SMS terms

These terms apply if you opt in to text messages from Studio.

- **Who we are:** Studio, by Nicolini Productions LLC. Texts come from Studio's registered SMS number, shown in your confirmation text.
- **What the texts are:** account notifications (for example, when your Studio invite is ready) if you check "Text me when my Studio invite is ready", and news and offers about Studio if you check "Text me Studio news and offers". Each is a separate, optional choice. Messages may be sent using automated technology.
- **Consent:** we text you only if you've checked an SMS consent box. **Consent is not a condition of any purchase.** You confirm that you are the subscriber or customary user of the number you provide.
- **Frequency:** message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month.
- **Cost:** **Message and data rates may apply.** Check with your mobile carrier.
- **Opt out:** **Reply STOP** to any message to stop all texts. We'll send one confirmation, and then no more texts unless you opt in again. You can also email hello@askstudio.ai.
- **Help:** **Reply HELP** for help, or contact support at hello@askstudio.ai.
- **Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.**
- **Privacy:** we use your number and consent record only to send you Studio texts, through our SMS provider Brevo. **Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy (this page).
- **Quiet hours:** we aim to send texts only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in your local time.

## 12. Security

We protect personal information with measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege access for staff, and reputable infrastructure providers. Payment card data is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS certified. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Protecting your Mac, your sign-in email and your connected accounts is up to you. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## 13. Children
